# No El Niño since 1950 has been this strong by September

How much warmer or cooler than normal the central Pacific was, season by season from June to May. The shaded area spans every year from 1950 to 2025; at +2.57°C for July to September, 2026 is above all of them.

How to read this. The orange line is this year, season by season. The grey lines are 1997–98 and 1982–83, the strongest El Niños on record, and the shaded area covers every year since 1950. Above the El Niño line the Pacific is warm enough to count as an El Niño; below the La Niña line, cool enough to count as a La Niña.

Sea-surface temperature in the Niño 3.4 region of the Pacific (5°N–5°S, 170°W–120°W), three-month averages against the 1991–2020 average, from Copernicus ERA5. El Niño conditions begin at +0.5°C. 2026 values are provisional (ERA5T).

Source: Copernicus Climate Change Service (ERA5); Zerodha calculations
