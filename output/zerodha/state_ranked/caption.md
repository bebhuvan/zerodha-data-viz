# Prices in Kerala are rising seven times as fast as in India overall

Year-on-year change in consumer prices, December 2025. Kerala's 9.49% compares with 1.33% for India; across most of the country prices barely moved. Manipur, at −4.23%, saw them fall.

How to read this. Each dot is a state or union territory, placed by how fast its prices rose in the year to December 2025. The line is India as a whole; the further a dot sits from it, the more that place differs.

Not shown, for want of a comparable figure: Ladakh, Arunachal Pradesh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.

Source: MOSPI (CPI, base 2012, combined rural and urban)
