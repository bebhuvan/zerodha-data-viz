# Gold beat every other asset in both 2024 and 2025

Calendar-year returns in rupees, 2016 to 2025, best first: gold, the Nifty 50 and four other things an Indian investor can buy. No other asset led two years running in this period.

How to read this. For each year, the six assets are listed from the best return to the worst. Gold is the orange cell and the Nifty 50 the blue-bordered one, so you can follow where each ranked year by year.

Price indices, so dividends are not counted. Gold is the COMEX price and the S&P 500 its index level, each turned into rupees at the year-end exchange rate; Dollar is the rupee price of one US dollar.

Source: NSE, CME and S&P via Yahoo Finance; Zerodha calculations
