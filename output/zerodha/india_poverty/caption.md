# Extreme poverty in India fell from 27% to under 3% in 12 years

Share of Indians below $3.00 a day (₹68 at 2023-24 prices), the World Bank's extreme poverty line: 37 million people, down from 344 million in 2011-12. At $8.30 (₹187), 80% still fall below.

Dollars are 2021 purchasing-power-parity dollars, converted at ₹19.47 a dollar and raised to 2023-24 prices. $4.20 (₹95) and $8.30 (₹187) are the lines for lower-middle and upper-middle-income countries. No national survey ran between 2011-12 and 2022-23: the dashed years are the World Bank's estimates from the surveys on either side and growth in the national accounts, which is why they rise in 2020, when the economy shrank. The newer survey records spending in more detail, so the two are not strictly comparable.

Source: World Bank, Poverty and Inequality Platform (September 2026 release); Zerodha calculations
