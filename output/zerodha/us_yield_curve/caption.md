# Long-term US interest rates are back above short-term ones

US Treasury yields by time to maturity, 30 October 2026 against 30 October 2023. Then the 10-year paid 4.88% and the 3-month 5.60%, an inverted curve; now the 10-year pays 4.94%.

How to read this. Each line shows what the US government pays to borrow, from three months on the left to 30 years on the right. A line that rises to the right is the usual shape; one that falls is called inverted.

Constant-maturity yields. The 10-year was last below the 3-month on 16 October 2025; its longest unbroken spell below ran 534 trading days, from 25 October 2022.

Source: US Federal Reserve (H.15) via FRED
