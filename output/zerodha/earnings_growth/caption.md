# Listed Indian companies' sales are growing at the fastest pace in three years

Growth from a year earlier in sales, EBITDA and net profit of BSE-listed companies, quarter by quarter. With results still coming in for Jul–Sep 2026, sales are up 20.6%, EBITDA 21.2% and net profit 26.9%.

How to read this. Each small chart is one measure: how much it grew from a year earlier, quarter by quarter. All three share the same scale. EBITDA is profit before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. The dashed last step is Jul–Sep 2026, with results still coming in.

Dashed: Jul–Sep 2026 counts only the companies that had announced results by 5 November 2026. The rest have until 14 November, so these figures may change. Growth compares the same companies a year apart.

Source: IndiaDataHub (all BSE-listed companies)
