# Vegetables got 18% cheaper in a year while personal care rose 28%

Year-on-year change in consumer prices, December 2024 and December 2025, for food groups and personal care. Vegetables went from the fastest rise to the steepest fall; personal care, which includes gold and silver, went the other way.

How to read this. Each line joins a group's inflation in December 2024, on the left, to December 2025, on the right. A line that falls means prices in that group are rising more slowly than a year ago, or falling.

MOSPI's published year-on-year rates for each CPI subgroup, All-India, rural and urban combined.

Source: MOSPI (CPI, base 2012)
