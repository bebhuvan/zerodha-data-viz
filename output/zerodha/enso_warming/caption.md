# La Niña years 2021 and 2022 were warmer than El Niño year 1998

World surface temperature each year against the 1991–2020 average, 1951 to 2025, by the state of the Pacific at the turn of the year: El Niño, La Niña or neutral. 1998: +0.02°C; 2021: +0.27°C; 2022: +0.30°C.

How to read this. Each column is one year's world temperature against the 1991–2020 average. La Niña years are usually cooler and El Niño years warmer, so blue columns standing above an orange one show the world warming underneath both.

A year takes the phase of its December–February Oceanic Niño Index (El Niño at +0.5 or more, La Niña at −0.5 or less). 2026 so far (January to September): +0.62°C, as a strong El Niño builds.

Source: Copernicus (ERA5) and NOAA via Our World in Data; Zerodha calculations
