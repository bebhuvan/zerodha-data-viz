# Cheaper food cut India's inflation from 5.22% to 1.33% in a year

Change in each group's contribution to year-on-year CPI inflation, December 2024 to December 2025, in percentage points.

How to read this. Start from last December's inflation. Each bar is one group of prices and shows how much it pushed inflation up or pulled it down over the year. Together they take you to this December's figure.

Contributions use MOSPI's 2012-base CPI weights and add up to headline inflation; bars use rates computed from group indices, within 0.03 points of the published ones. Everything else covers clothing, transport, health, education and the rest.

Source: MOSPI (CPI, base 2012, All-India combined), Zerodha calculations
