# Food makes up 46% of India's consumer price index

Weights of the CPI basket, All-India, rural and urban combined, out of 100. Personal care, which includes gold and silver, is under 4%; it moves the index only when its prices move a lot.

How to read this. The grid has 100 squares, one for each point of weight in the price index. Food fills 46 of them, so a change in food prices moves the index more than anything else.

Weights from MOSPI's 2012 base, drawn from the 2011–12 household consumption survey. Each square is one point of weight.

Source: MOSPI (CPI, base 2012)
