# India's inflation fell to 0.25% in October 2025, the lowest on record

Year-on-year change in consumer prices, every month from January 2014 to December 2025, coloured by where it sits against the Reserve Bank's target band of 2% to 6%.

How to read this. Each square is one month, with years running one way and months the other. Grey means inflation was inside the RBI's band of 2% to 6%, blue below it and orange above it. Records begin in January 2014.

The RBI's inflation target is 4%, with a band of 2% to 6%, in force since August 2016. Dashed cells: April and May 2020, when MOSPI published no rate.

Source: MOSPI (CPI, base 2012, All-India combined); RBI
