# No El Niño since 1950 has been this strong by September

How much warmer than normal the central Pacific was in July to September each year, 1950 to 2026. 2026 is ahead of 1997 (+1.97°C) and 2015 (+1.79°C), the next strongest at this time of year.

How to read this. Each column is one year: how far the central Pacific's surface was above or below its normal temperature from July to September. Above the El Niño line it is warm enough to count as an El Niño; below the La Niña line, cool enough to count as a La Niña.

Sea-surface temperature in the Niño 3.4 region of the Pacific (5°N–5°S, 170°W–120°W), three-month averages against the 1991–2020 average, from Copernicus ERA5. El Niño conditions begin at +0.5°C. 2026 values are provisional (ERA5T).

Source: Copernicus Climate Change Service (ERA5); Zerodha calculations
