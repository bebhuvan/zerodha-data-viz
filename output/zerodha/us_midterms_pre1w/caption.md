# Nifty 50 rose in the week before nine of ten US mid-term elections

Nifty 50 change in the seven days up to each US mid-term election, 1990 to 2026. 2026 gained +4.7% before the vote and +0.6% on results day; only 1998 (−2.5%) fell in the run-up.

Price index (dividends excluded), measured over the seven calendar days ending on the last Indian close on or before US Election Tuesday. Across the nine completed cycles since 1990, the median gain in the week after the vote slowed to +0.5%.
