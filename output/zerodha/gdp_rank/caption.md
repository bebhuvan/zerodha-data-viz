# India rose from 12th to 5th largest economy and is now 6th

Rank by GDP in US dollars, 2004 to 2025, for every economy that made the top ten. India was 5th from 2022 to 2024; in 2025 the UK moved back ahead of it.

How to read this. Each line follows one economy's rank year by year, with 1st at the top. India's line climbs from 12th to 5th, then drops a place in 2025.

At market exchange rates, so a weaker rupee lowers India's dollar GDP even when the economy grows. In 2025 India's GDP was $3.96 trillion.

Source: World Bank, World Development Indicators
