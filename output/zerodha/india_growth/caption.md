# India's economy has shrunk in only five years since 1961

Real GDP growth each year, 1961 to 2025. The economy shrank in 1965, 1966, 1972, 1979 and 2020. Since 1991 growth has averaged 6.1% a year, against 4.2% before.

Calendar-year figures in constant prices as published by the World Bank; India's own data runs April to March.

Source: World Bank, World Development Indicators
