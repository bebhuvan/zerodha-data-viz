# Prices in Kerala are rising seven times as fast as in India overall

Year-on-year change in consumer prices, December 2025. Kerala's 9.49% compares with 1.33% for India; across most of the country prices barely moved. Manipur, at −4.23%, saw them fall.

How to read this. Each square is a state or union territory, placed roughly where it sits on the map. Its colour shows how fast prices rose there in the year to December 2025.

Dashed outline: no comparable figure. Ladakh: no separate CPI series; Arunachal Pradesh: no combined index published; Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu: CPI still reports the two former UTs separately.

Source: MOSPI (CPI, base 2012, combined rural and urban)
