# India is the fastest-growing large economy and the poorest per person

Real GDP growth in 2025 against GDP per person in US dollars, on a log scale, for the 20 largest economies. India: $2,702 a person, 7.6% growth.

How to read this. Each dot is one economy. Further right means richer per person; higher up means faster growth. India sits alone at the top left.

GDP per person at market exchange rates; a log scale gives each tenfold step the same width.

Source: World Bank, World Development Indicators
