# The Nifty's 10.1% fall from its peak is mild by past standards

How far the Nifty 50 stood below its highest close up to that day, September 2007 to 3 November 2026. The index last peaked in January 2026. It took 24 months to climb back after the 2008 low and 8 after 2020's.

How to read this. The line shows how far the Nifty stood below its highest close so far. Zero means a new high; the deeper the dip, the bigger the fall.

Daily closes of the price index, so dividends are not counted.
