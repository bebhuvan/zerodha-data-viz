# Falling food prices have pulled India's inflation down to 1.33%

Contribution to year-on-year CPI inflation, percentage points, January 2022 to December 2025. In December food took off 0.89 points while personal care, which includes gold and silver, added 1.16.

How to read this. Each bar is one month's inflation, split into what each group of prices added to it. Parts above zero pushed inflation up; parts below zero pulled it down.

Contributions use MOSPI's 2012-base CPI weights and add up to headline inflation. Everything else includes housing, fuel, clothing, transport, health and education.

Source: MOSPI (CPI, base 2012, All-India combined), Zerodha calculations
