# Personal care prices are up 28%, far faster than any other group

Year-on-year change in consumer prices for each CPI group, monthly, 2014 to 2025; the other groups in grey. Personal care includes gold and silver jewellery.

How to read this. Each small chart is one group of prices, drawn on the same scale, with the other seven groups in grey behind it for comparison.
